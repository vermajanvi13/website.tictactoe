How To Play

Learn the rules in just a few seconds.

1

Choose X or O and start the game.

2

Take turns placing your symbol on an empty square.

3

Get three of your symbols in a row, column, or diagonal.

4

The first player to complete a line wins.

5

If all nine squares are filled without a winner, the game is a draw.

About The Game
